Verify a Veklom receipt

Checks a CAPPO execution receipt (COSE_Sign1, Ed25519) against the published key, without trusting CAPPO.

The key id (kid) travels outside the signature, so it is checked separately: it must be the SHA-256 fingerprint of the key that verified the signature.

Paste a receipt bundle and the published key, then press Verify.

Checks

    Signed fields

    Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.
